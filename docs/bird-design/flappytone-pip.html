The Pip — your round dot keeps its body but gains a beak & eye. The beak tip is the pitch point (the only thing scoring reads); the body trails behind it. It tilts to lean into the tone.

Anatomy

Tilts with the tone

Rising ˊ — beak up
Flat ˉ — level
Falling ˋ — beak down

States

Flying (default)
Nailed it (pop + glow)
Couldn't hear (faded, neutral)
